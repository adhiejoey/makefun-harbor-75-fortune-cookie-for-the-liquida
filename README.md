One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated: it
keeps the cookie's own voice — the lucky numbers printed on the back of every
fortune — and turns them into the punchline, because the trader's lucky
numbers are all zeroes, exactly matching a balance wiped out by liquidation.
Rueful rather than cruel, it reads whole in a single breath the way a real
fortune does.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the fortune to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output`), after checking it against the gate itself: 1–160
characters and at most 20 words.
